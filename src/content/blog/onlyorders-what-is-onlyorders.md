If you sell on Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, or Instagram, you have likely wished the conversation between "Hello, is this in stock?" and "Order confirmed" took less of your day. **OnlyOrders** is a product built to compress exactly that distance.

This post is a plain-language introduction, based on the product's published positioning and features on its official site at [onlyorders.tech](https://onlyorders.tech).

## The core idea

OnlyOrders describes itself, in its own words, as an AI commerce autopilot that **turns DMs and comments into confirmed orders**. The positioning sits within "conversational commerce": the sale happens inside the chat where the buyer already is, rather than on a separate cart page.

The product tagline is **"Your orders, on autopilot."** The pitch is that a store can answer customers instantly, keep every channel in one shared inbox, let buyers check out inside the chat, and publish posts on schedule — all from one place.

## What it does

The four headline feature blocks, as public on the product site, are:

- **Instant replies** — AI answers customers 24/7.
- **One inbox** — every channel in one place, no tab chaos.
- **Chat to order** — from receipt through to courier handoff.
- **Auto posts** — drops published on schedule.

The hero line summarizes it as: *everything a chat-first store needs, in one place.*

## Who it is for

OnlyOrders is aimed at social sellers and chat-first stores — small businesses that take orders through Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, or Instagram. The product's demo categories (kurti, three-piece, saree, panjabi) and its support for mobile wallets like bKash and Nagad reflect markets such as Bangladesh, where messaging is a primary selling channel and mobile payments are the norm.

## A note on the brand

OnlyOrders is the current name of this product. Its website previously displayed an older brand name in places, but the product is OnlyOrders — that is the name you should look for. We mention this only to help you avoid confusion if you come across older materials.
