OnlyOrders' published workflow runs from **first message to delivered order** in four steps. This post walks through each one so you can see how a chat-first sale is supposed to flow, based on the product's documentation at [onlyorders.tech](https://onlyorders.tech).

## Step 1: Messenger AI replies

The flow starts when a customer messages the store. The AI answers price, size, and stock questions in seconds, in the shop's tone — and hands off to a human when the conversation gets specific, sensitive, or emotional. This is the front line that absorbs the repetitive questions.

## Step 2: Checkout inside the chat

The buyer confirms the cart, address, and payment method **without leaving Messenger**. This is the crucial friction-removal step: the purchase is captured in the conversation itself, so the customer does not have to be steered off to a separate checkout page.

## Step 3: One orders dashboard

Every chat order lands in a single board with status, notes, and totals across all channels. This gives the seller a unified view of the business instead of a scattered pile of threads.

## Step 4: Courier tracking, auto-shared

The seller books the courier in one tap, and tracking updates are sent back to the buyer's chat automatically. The loop closes where it started — in the conversation.

## The demo exchange

The product's UI shows a representative exchange:

> Customer: "Is the blue kurti available in M?"
> AI: "Yes — 3 left in M. Want me to reserve one?"
> Customer: "Yes please, cash on delivery."
> AI: "Done. Order #OO-2481 is confirmed ✓"

The demo also shows a "salaam" greeting, category suggestions (kurti / three-piece / saree / panjabi), photo match, size check, receipt, coupon, and review.

## What this tells you

The four-step flow is a clean illustration of the chat-first thesis: confirm, verify, pack, inform — all anchored in the same conversation. The dashboard unifies channels, and the courier step closes the loop with the buyer. If you are evaluating chat-commerce tooling, these are the operational stages to probe: Does it capture order details from the chat? Does it verify the payment method your buyers use? Does it book the courier and share tracking back?

That is the practical test of any tool in this category, and it is exactly the flow OnlyOrders is built around.
